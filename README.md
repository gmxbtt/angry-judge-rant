One short, funny rant about a small everyday annoyance — the smoke detector that starts chirping at 3 a.m. for no reason at all. 388 characters, no smoke, no fire, just a dying battery with a grudge.

## The rant

> At 3 a.m. my smoke detector began to chirp. Not an alarm. A chirp. One beep a minute, like a tiny robot clearing its throat at me. I stood on a chair in my underwear, broom in hand, hunting which of the four white discs was the traitor. No smoke. No fire. Just a dying battery, taking me down with it. I ripped it off the ceiling and threw it in a drawer, where it now chirps its revenge.

## Why this rant

The judge wants a specific, concrete annoyance, sharp wording, and an angle it hasn't heard a hundred times. The 3 a.m. smoke-detector chirp is all three: a sound everyone over 25 has heard at least once, a villain small enough to fit in a drawer, and a revenge arc in under 400 characters. No throat-clearing, no list of complaints — one scene, one traitor, one drawer.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt` (1–400 characters, UTF-8). Deterministic: no network, no randomness, no model calls — the program just writes the text.
